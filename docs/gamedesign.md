# Game Design

## Ten Game Design principle 

1. Clear Objective and Goals : Player motivation
2. Rules: What players can and cannot do
3. Core Loop  gameplay : what players do over and over
4. Feedback : Rewards after the core loop gameplay, such as leveling and achievements, letting players know if they did the right or wrong thing
5. Flow : Balancing challenge and skill by making the game more challenging, ramping up difficulty, but also giving more abilities
6. Balance : The sweet spot between too hard and too easy
7. Risk vs Reward : Players need to make decisions to make the gameplay more meaningful
8. Meaning: Creating experiences where players feel their actions or decisions have a significant impact
9. Immersion : Pulls players into the game with powerful gameplay, beautiful visual design, or an amazing narrative, what makes them feel part of the game
10. Fun : Well, it's subjective

In conclusion, first, start with a clear objective and define the rules that shape your game world. Then, create engaging core mechanics and ensure there is a satisfying game loop supported by strong feedback. Keep players in a state of flow with balanced challenges and make them weigh risk and reward. Infuse your game with meaning, craft an immersive world, and above all, make sure the experience is fun for your target audience. [^1]
